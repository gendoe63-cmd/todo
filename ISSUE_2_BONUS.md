**Title:** "tasks remaining" counter shows the wrong number

**Body:**

### What happens
The counter under the list shows how many tasks are *finished*, not how many are left.

### Steps to reproduce
1. Open `index.html` and add three tasks.
2. Look at the counter. It says "0 tasks remaining" (should be 3).
3. Complete one task. It says "1 task remaining" (should be 2).

### Expected
Counter shows the number of tasks that are not done.
